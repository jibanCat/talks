The Lyman-alpha Forest

Reading the Universe and Galaxies in Shadows

A ten-minute talk for a general audience, given at the Leinweber Foundation's site visit to the University of Michigan on 29 October 2026.

It shows how the light of distant quasars, crossing the hydrogen between galaxies, maps the Universe's expansion with DESI's standard ruler and reaches down to the smallest scales, where dark matter's properties start to show. Then it follows one quasar's light through a PRIYA cosmological simulation, cloud by cloud.
